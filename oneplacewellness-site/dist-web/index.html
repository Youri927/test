Three to five millimeters of shadow can add up to five years to a face.

That is why she doesn't simply puff up the cheeks or fill the folds beside the mouth. She reads the whole face: the expression lines, a downturned mouth, the brow, the strength of the jaw. Then she softens the shadows that make a face look tired, sad or older than it feels.

The aim is to restore, not to overdo: subtle changes that make a real difference, and still look like you.

    For the nasolabial folds, the lines around the lips, the lips, the cheeks, the jawline and the jowls. Fillers need precision: symmetry, the existing anatomy and the structures that hold the face up.

    Jowl reduction is Dr. Fox's specialty. Adding definition along the side of the jaw makes the jowls recede and the lower face look leaner, and it can lift the neck: a liquid face lift. Done well, fillers can even make a face look leaner: the opposite of "Hollywood lips".

    Many people expect fillers to last 6 to 12 months; they can last much longer, and later visits only add what aging takes away. Fillers can delay, or even prevent, the need for a facelift.

    Sutures placed under the skin lift the sagging mid-face right away, then restore volume gradually as they stimulate new collagen. Threads can also tighten loose skin on the neck, as an alternative to facial surgery.

    Dr. Fox explains where each suture will go and marks the face with a pen, then numbs the area. The procedure takes 45 minutes. Expect minor soreness, swelling or bruising; most patients are back to their routine within days.

    The maker reports results lasting up to two years; Dr. Fox's patients have kept theirs longer. Twenty-five years of facelifts are why she knows exactly where a thread should sit.

    Silhouette InstaLift®

  4. No downtime, a series of three Final results at 3 to 6 months A year or longer, with upkeep every 9 to 12 months
    • No downtime; three treatments are recommended
    • A first difference after the first treatment
    • Final results at 3 to 6 months

    Radiofrequency warms the skin to a temperature Dr. Fox sets and controls. The heat tightens existing collagen, brings more blood to the skin and stimulates new collagen, which fills fine lines and smooths the skin. For jowls, loose neck skin, neck bands and the jawline, and also the arms.

    A little gel, then the handpiece moves in sweeping or circular motions. There is no downtime. Most patients see a difference after the first treatment; three are recommended, and the final result arrives three to six months later. It can last a year or longer, kept up with one treatment every 9 to 12 months.

    It is subtle, not a facelift: best for some laxity, fine lines and wrinkles. Combined with fillers and Botox, and with a retinol (vitamin A) product at home, it becomes part of a routine.

    ThermiSmooth

  5. About 15 minutes Healed in 3 days: the weekend procedure
    • Numbing cream 30 minutes before, about 15 minutes of laser
    • More than 98% of patients healed in 3 days
    • Stop retinoids 3 days before

    Florida sun shows on the skin. The MicroLaser Peel treats fine lines, sun spots and pigmentation, texture, tone, acne scars, rosacea and melasma, on the face, the neck, the hands, the chest and the shoulders, and sun spots on the legs. Its depth spares the layer where the skin's pigment cells live, so your natural color stays intact.

    Numbing cream goes on 30 minutes before, and cool air flows during the treatment: most patients feel a slight stinging. The laser itself takes about 15 minutes. More than 98% of patients are healed in three days, with makeup on and back out in public, which is why the practice calls it the weekend procedure. Stop retinoids three days before.

    For comparison, the practice cites four to six weeks of recovery for a CO2 laser, one to two weeks for fractional lasers and about seven days for a chemical peel.

Elizabeth Fox, M.D.

A facelift surgeon's eye, without the surgery.

Dr. Fox is a plastic surgeon who has specialized in facelift surgery for more than 25 years and has treated more than 9,000 patients. She knows the face in three dimensions, and where its shadows come from: the bone, the fat and the firmness it loses with age.

That is what tells her where a filler, a thread or a dose of Botox will look natural, and last.

  • Radiesse chose her as a medical faculty advisor, to train other doctors.
  • She pioneered a non-surgical neck lift: filler along the jaw, Botox for the neck bands, ThermiSmooth to tighten.
  • Her patients range in age from 20 to 90.
  • Every first Botox treatment comes with a complimentary 30-unit booster two weeks later.

Patients often come back to tell her what friends and family keep asking: why do you look so young?

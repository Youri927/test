New homes, from the lot up.

Already own a lot, or looking for one? Before anything is drawn, we look at what the land allows, then stay on the job until you move in.

We build in new communities and on individual home sites. We’ll work with your architect and designer, or you can work with ours. From the first design to the final walk-through, Chris and Mike Skoda and their crew are on site.

  • Are the utilities and the soil ready for the build?

Better than new.

When a hurricane, wind or water damages your home, we rebuild it to the standard it had before the storm.

Luxury homes have finishes that are hard to match. We match what survived, inside and out: stucco, paint, wallpaper, trim, banisters, staircases, patio stones. When a piece can’t be found, we have it made.

Nov 2022Dec 2023

  1. November 2022

    The house after the storm

    Debris piled out front, rooms wrecked on the ground floor, the space under the house torn up.

  2. Drying out

    Walls open, fans running

    The wet drywall cut away at the bottom, the studs exposed, air movers and dehumidifiers on every floor.

  3. December 2023

    The same house, rebuilt

    New siding and trim, the arched windows, white railings up the stairs, new landscaping and a stone path.

How an office build-out runs

  1. We listen to what your business needs the space to do.
  2. We work within your budget and your schedule.
  3. We bring design ideas and practical solutions for the layout.
  4. We assess the space and estimate the cost.
  5. We settle the details first: air conditioning, restrooms, break rooms, lighting.
  6. We stay with it from planning to completion.
  7. We pull the permits and schedule inspections on time.

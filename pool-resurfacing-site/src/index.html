04 How it goes

From drain to first swim.

Most straightforward resurfacing projects take 3 to 4 days. Here is everything that happens, in order.

FullEmpty

Old surface

  1. Step 01 · Day 1

    Drain, dry, dust

    We drain the pool and inspect it from top to bottom. Hoses and pumps are checked, and any furniture at risk is noted before work starts.

  2. Step 02

    Preparation

    Everything around the pool is covered. The old surface is stripped, then sandblasted so the new finish bonds. Sandblasting makes dust: we keep it down and clean it all up.

  3. Step 03 · 1 to 3 days

    Masonry and tile

    Decorative features and tile go in. Before we start, you approve the final patterns and locations, and we document it in writing.

  4. Step 04 · 1 day

    Sealant and plumbing

    A full day to make sure the plumbing is sealed and watertight, with any repairs it needs.

  5. Step 05 · 1 to 3 days

    The new finish

    The finish you chose goes on. Quartz, Hydrazzo, pebble and Beadcrete then get a thorough acid wash by specialized crew.

  6. Step 06

    Fill and clean-up

    One last general clean-up, then we fill the pool. Be around to turn the water off.

  7. Step 07 · up to 7 days

    First water treatment

    Several checks and adjustments to cure the new surface. We do it, or your own pool service does: your choice.

06 Questions

Straight answers.

Anything else, call or text 480-568-1704. We are always upfront about prices and what to expect.

What does pool resurfacing involve?

Over time a pool’s walls and floor deteriorate. Instead of building a new pool, resurfacing replaces the worn-out surface with a new protective layer.

How often should I resurface my pool?

A concrete, marcite or gunite surface lasts around 15 years. Leaves, tree roots, excessive chemical use and more can bring some pools to resurfacing in as little as 10 years. A new resurface usually lasts 10 to 20+ years, depending on the materials, the workmanship, water chemistry and UV.

How long does it take?

Most straightforward resurfacing projects take 3 to 4 days. A remodel averages two to four weeks, depending on the scope.

How fast can you come out?

We can often send a pool technician to your home the same day you call. After the assessment and consultation, you receive a formal quote the very next day.

When is the best time of year?

Scottsdale’s weather allows resurfacing all year. To avoid the summer rush, plan your project for the slower winter months.

How much does resurfacing cost?

Every pool is different. Size matters most, then the shape (more curves, more intricate work) and the material: plain or colored concrete costs the least, pebble the most, and custom patterns add to it. Have your pool’s dimensions ready, and photos if you can, for the most accurate quote.

Which finish lasts the longest for the money?

Diamond Brite is affordable, sturdy and looks great. It usually lasts a little over a decade, with a single acid wash in that time. Pebble finishes last up to 20 years when maintained properly.

Do you offer warranties?

Yes. Our pricing includes an experienced crew, insurance and warranty options. We are licensed, bonded and insured.

Where a facial surgeon’s training shows.

Four procedures that rely on the training Dr. Lee received on the face, the nose and the eyelids.

Jaw contouring

A softer jawline, two ways.

When bone gives the jaw its width, V-line jaw reduction reshapes it, reducing both the angle and the width for a slimmer, heart-shaped lower face. When the masseter muscle is the cause, BOTOX® slims the jaw over time, without surgery.

Dr. Lee introduced to North America the use of BOTOX® for a more slender jawline, and to reduce teeth grinding.

Eyelid surgery

A crease, not a new face.

Blepharoplasty rejuvenates the upper and lower eyelids. Many Asian patients have no crease on the upper eyelid; for those who would like one, Dr. Lee creates a crease that looks natural, without altering any other ethnic features.

Trained in Asian cosmetic surgery with Robert Flowers, MD, in Honolulu. Has taught Asian plastic surgery courses for the American Society for Aesthetic Plastic Surgery.

Rhinoplasty

Form, and the air through it.

Rhinoplasty improves the size and shape of the nose, and can correct breathing problems. Open rhinoplasty allows more complex reshaping; closed techniques mean less recovery time and no external scar.

Five years’ training on the internal structures of the face and nose, on top of four years in plastic and reconstructive surgery.

Endoscopic facelift

A lift through the smallest door.

Small incisions hidden along the hairline or around the ears, and a tiny camera: the endoscopic facelift lifts the midface and cheeks with a less invasive approach than a traditional facelift, and a faster recovery.

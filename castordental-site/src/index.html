The wire goes behind your teeth.

Stealth Orthodontics™ straightens teeth with super-elastic wires bonded to their side, sometimes with custom clear trays. From the front, people see your teeth.

A patient of the practice, photographed from the tongue side, the one side where the wire shows. Treatment took five months.

What does it cost?
In most cases, about half the cost of traditional braces.
How long does it take?
Generally within six months, depending on how complex the case is. Every patient, and every case, is different.
Who can have it?
People of any age. Because it is concealed, it suits everyone from the most active teenager to the business professional.

Two other ways to straighten teeth, for which Dr. Castor is certified:

Invisalign®

Clear aligners made for you in SmartTrack® material, nearly invisible. You move to a new set, typically every week, following a plan Dr. Castor draws up for your teeth.

Six Month Smiles®

For more advanced cases: clear Lucid-Lok® brackets and tooth-colored wires, with gentle forces. Treatment averages six months, and it usually costs less than traditional braces, aligners or veneers.

Keeping teeth healthy

CleaningsWith one of four registered dental hygienists.

Brushing and flossing every day slow down tartar; a professional cleaning removes what they miss, above and below the gumline (), then polishes the teeth (). Regular cleanings are a key part of preventing gum disease, which, past age 35, costs adults more teeth than cavities do.

FillingsTooth-colored, in composite resin.

Cavities are filled in , the color of the tooth. The same material repairs chipped edges: see bonding, below.

Children's dentistryThe first visit, just after the third birthday.

The first visit is short. The dentist gently checks the teeth and gums, may take X-rays to see how the adult teeth are coming in, cleans the teeth and applies . Parents get a cleaning routine and a schedule for the next visits.

To prepare your child, treat it like a first haircut: visit the office beforehand, read a book about the dentist together, and talk about your own visits in a positive way. Later, protect the decay-prone back teeth.

Restorations that look natural

Crowns and bridgesPorcelain, made to look like your own teeth.

“Most dentistry, looks like dentistry. Our goal is to provide dentistry that is undetectable.” Old crowns and fillings are replaced with restorations that look and feel like your natural teeth.

A covers a misshapen or badly broken tooth; a replaces missing teeth. Both are made in high-grade porcelain or porcelain bonded to gold, and fix both the look of the smile and chewing.

VeneersA thin layer over the front of the tooth.

A improves the look of a tooth and protects its surface. In composite, it is shaped directly on the tooth or made in a lab; in porcelain, it is always made in a lab, then bonded to the tooth.

BondingA chipped or stained tooth, repaired in one visit.

An alternative to veneers for a tooth that is chipped, cracked, discolored or out of line. The surface is lightly etched, then composite resin is sculpted on, trimmed and polished to look natural.

It is often done in a single visit. The resin is not as strong as enamel and can stain or chip: it typically lasts three to five years before it needs repair.

WhiteningCustom trays, ready in one to two days.

treats brown, yellow and spotted discoloration, at any age. The practice makes clear trays to fit your teeth and gives you the whitening gel; you wear them a short time each day at home to reach the best result.

Laser dentistryDr. Castor is certified in laser dentistry.

Replacing teeth

ImplantsA titanium root, then a new tooth. Six to eight months.

An is a small titanium post placed in the jawbone where a tooth is missing. The bone bonds to it, which also keeps the jaw from shrinking, and it carries a replacement tooth that looks, feels and works like your own.

  1. An exam, X-rays and your health history.
  2. An oral and maxillofacial surgeon, referred by the practice, places the implant. It heals under the gum for three to six months while it bonds with the bone.
  3. The surgeon uncovers it; Dr. Castor takes an and makes the new tooth, designed during the healing.

The whole process usually takes six to eight months. Dr. Castor has worked with most implant systems.

DenturesComplete or partial.

A replaces missing teeth and the tissue around them. There are many types, chosen for your mouth and your preferences.

Shown on a screen, not just described

An intraoral camera, sometimes only a few millimeters long, shows your teeth and gums on a screen, in detail a mirror can miss. Digital X-rays () use a sixth of the radiation of film, and can be sent quickly to a specialist or your insurer. Impressions can be taken with a digital scanner.

Good to know.

How long does it take to build a pool?

From dig to deck: 3 to 3½ weeks for a concrete pool, 2 to 2½ weeks for a vinyl liner pool, and about two weeks for a fiberglass pool.

Do you take care of the permits?

Yes. We handle permitting, excavation, decking, electrical work, screen enclosures, landscaping, low-voltage lighting, irrigation and retaining walls.

Can you add a spa to my existing pool?

Yes. We can install a spa or hot tub in your existing pool area, or build a new spa with its own landscaping and paver deck.

Do you repair fiberglass, vinyl and concrete pools?

Yes. We repair cracked, faded or worn fiberglass, replace ripped or rotted vinyl liners, and renovate cracking or deteriorating concrete and gunite pools.

Do you offer financing?

Yes, through HFS Financial and Lyon Financial. See financing.

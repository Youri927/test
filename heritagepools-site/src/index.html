What we do

From a new pool to the care of the one you already have.

  • New pools

    We design your pool and build it, start to finish.

  • Inspections

    Pool inspections by licensed pool inspectors.

  • Repairs

    Repairs for the pool you already have.

  • Renovation

    Renovations that bring an older pool up to date.

  • Re-plaster

    A new interior finish when the old plaster is worn.

  • Maintenance

    Care and upkeep once your pool is built.

With Heritage Pools TX on your side, rest assured that you and your pool are in good hands.

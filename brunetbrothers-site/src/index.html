From the first visit to the last payment.

  1. Do you charge for estimates?

    No. We come to your home, look at the room and take notes and measurements. Then you get a detailed proposal: what’s included in the scope of work, for the price quoted.

  2. How long will my project take?

    It depends on the size and scope of the job. Before work starts, a construction schedule lists each step, so you can see how things should be progressing.

  3. Do I need permits?

    Most remodels do. When yours does, we get the permit, handle all the inspections and make sure everything is built to code.

  4. Can I stay in my home during the work?

    For most upgrades, yes. A complete renovation may mean staying somewhere else for a while.

  5. What is the payment process?

    Typically a 50% deposit to begin. Then payments at points set in the contract, and the final payment when the work is complete.

  6. What is a change order?

    A written statement you sign for extra work that isn’t in the contract, or for damage found along the way. Its amount is due at completion.

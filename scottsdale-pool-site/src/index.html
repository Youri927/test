(08) Questions

Good to know before we dig.

Something else on your mind? Call 602-926-2950

Every project is different, but typical pool, patio and landscape projects range from about $100,000 to $1 million and up. Setting a budget early keeps everyone on the same page, tells us what can and cannot be built, and guides material choices.

The average project takes 12 to 16 weeks. More elaborate projects can take 6 to 12 months or more, depending on the design, location, size, features and complexity.

A single point of contact. You will often be updated daily on progress, and at a minimum two to three times a week. We are always here to answer questions as they come up.

Almost every city has its own rules for a safety barrier around a pool, and some sites call for different perimeter protection. Your designer will explain your city's requirements and every option available to you.

It depends on the site. We use small equipment that fits through walk-in gates. When necessary, we take down a section of wall or gate to dig the pool and replace it afterwards.

A difficult site brings out the best in our designer. We specialize in unique designs for unique situations, and within each city's setbacks and codes, almost anything is possible.

A properly designed pool should need less than 15 to 20 minutes of maintenance a week, by yourself or a professional. After filling, we take you through a start-up orientation and provide initial chemicals, a brush, pole, net and test kit.

Every project is unique, and our competitive design fee reflects the detailed scope of work. The first consultation is free, so we can start from there.

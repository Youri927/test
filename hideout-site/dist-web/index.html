What we do

Five families of treatments under one roof. Every plan starts with a consultation and pairs care in our rooms with care at home.

    • SkinPen

Before your visit

  • Arrive 15 minutes early. Your forms are emailed when you book; filling them out ahead saves time.
  • Come with little or no makeup. For lashes, skip mascara for two days before.
  • Need to cancel? Let us know at least 24 hours ahead. Late cancellations lose the booking fee or are charged 50% of the service, and no-shows 100%.
  • Running late? After 15 minutes, we may need to reschedule.

Nervous about the dentist? Tell us.

Dental anxiety affects all kinds of people, and it can even keep the numbing from working. We listen first, then you choose how much help you’d like.

  1. For everyone

    Gentle, unhurried care

    We answer every question and explain your options before anything happens. There’s a TV in every room.

  2. A little nervous

    Nitrous oxide

    “Laughing gas” through a small mask: the anxiety fades and the numbing works as it should. It wears off right away, so you can drive home.

  3. Really anxious

    Oral conscious sedation

    A pill before your visit. You stay conscious and relaxed while we monitor your vital signs, and you likely won’t remember the procedure. Someone drives you home.

Your first visit

Less than an hour, and often treatment the same day.

  1. Welcome

    Arrive about 15 minutes early for the new patient form, or fill it out online.

  2. Exam

    X-rays, then a complete exam of your teeth, gums and oral tissues with your dentist. Every option explained.

  3. Cleaning

    A good, thorough cleaning.

  4. Your plan

    If you need treatment, our coordinator walks you through it. We can often schedule it the same day.

ProfileThe artist03

Profile · Dr. Andrea Cameron

“She considers every smile she designs or renews to be a work of art.”

Growing up near Toronto, Andrea Cameron found her calling at ten. By sixteen she was spending every summer in a dental office, learning all she could. She went on to graduate with honors from the University of Toronto’s Faculty of Dentistry, collecting awards along the way.

Today she works where medical science meets art: restoring healthy function and aesthetics, and with them confidence. Smile makeovers are her favorite work. Outside the studio she makes art of other kinds: detailed ceramics, expansive wall murals, clothing design.

Trust is the cornerstone of her practice. She explains, she offers options, and she leaves the decision to you, in a no-pressure environment.

AdviceComfort06

Nervous? You’re in good company.

Dental anxiety affects all kinds of people, and it can even keep the numbing from working. We listen first. Then you choose how much help you’d like.

I’m fineA little nervousReally anxious

Our gentle approach

Gentle, unhurried care.

What we do
Listen, answer every question, and explain your options before anything happens.
In the room
A TV in every operatory: watch whatever helps you feel comfortable.
Patients say
“She is gentle and comforting when drilling.” — Laura Barsamian

For mild anxiety

Nitrous oxide, “laughing gas”.

How it feels
A carefree feeling through a mask over your nose, while the anxiety fades.
Why it helps
Calm lets the numbing work as it should, for a pain-free treatment.
Afterwards
It wears off as soon as you stop breathing it in. You can drive yourself home.

For stronger anxiety

Oral conscious sedation.

How it feels
A pill before your appointment. Completely relaxed, still conscious, with your natural reflexes.
Safety
We monitor your vital signs and oxygen levels throughout.
Afterwards
You likely won’t remember the procedure. Someone will need to drive you home.

Five doctors, one studio.

Cosmetic artistry, gum surgery, periodontics, oral surgery and family dentistry, under one roof, with the convenience of in-house implant surgery.

  • Honors graduate of the University of Toronto, with post-graduate training in cosmetic dentistry and certifications in Invisalign and Botox. Top Dentist, Naples Illustrated, 2021.

  • Top of his class at Western Ontario. Awards from the American Academy of Periodontology and the American Academy of Endodontics. Mentors students, and consults for Florida Dental Board Preps.

  • Nearly two decades of experience. Gum disease surgery, dental implants and bone regeneration, with minimally invasive techniques. MS in Periodontics, Nova Southeastern University.

  • Over 20 years in surgical dentistry: complex implants, bone grafting, extractions, full-mouth rehabilitation. Residency at UNAM, training at the Eastman Institute. Fellow of the IAOMS.

  • DDS and MS in Biomaterials, University at Buffalo; Advanced Education in General Dentistry in Southwest Florida. A particular interest in restorative and removable dentistry.

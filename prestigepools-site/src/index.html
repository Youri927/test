Made in one piece. Set in days.

A Latham fiberglass pool is built at the factory as a single shell. We bring it to your yard, set it in place and finish everything around it.

3 to 5 days
to install a fiberglass pool, against 4 to 8 weeks for concrete.
Smooth to the touch
No scraped feet or knees. The surface is non-porous, so it needs fewer chemicals and resists algae.
Built to flex
Fiberglass bends instead of cracking through freeze and thaw.
Lifetime warranty
on the structure of the pool, and 20 years on the surface.

Shane has built concrete, vinyl liner and fiberglass pools since 1991. Today Prestige installs one kind only: Latham fiberglass.

Our machines. Our crews.

We own the dump trucks, excavators, track loaders and trailers that build your pool, so we can schedule your build more efficiently. Our construction crews have been with the Prestige family for more than 12 years.

  1. Layout

    The pool layout is carefully marked on your lawn.

  2. Dig

    The dig begins.

  3. Shape

    The hole is prepared to the exact shape and depth of your pool.

  4. Set

    The shell is set in place.

  5. Fill

    The pool is filled with water as the backfill begins.

  6. Wire

    Plumbing and electrical equipment go in, under Shane’s pool electrical license.

  7. Deck

    The concrete is poured around the pool.

  8. Swim

    Your Latham pool is ready, with its lifetime warranty on the structure.

Small yard? The cocktail pool.

Our Latham cocktail pool measures 10 by 16 feet, with one full-width set of steps and benches all the way around, so there is room to sit for everyone. It is ready for an automatic cover, costs less to heat and maintain than a full-size pool, and can be heated like a hot tub for early spring and late fall.

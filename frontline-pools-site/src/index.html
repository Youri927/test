What we rebuild

Six parts of a pool, one licensed crew. Scroll, and we’ll show you where each one is.

Scroll to look closer

The finish

The surface your feet touch, and the color your water turns. We resurface with StoneScapes aggregate finishes by NPT, in regular or mini pebble, and help you choose the color.

ResurfacingStoneScapes by NPTPebble, mini pebble

The waterline

The six-inch band that takes the beating: calcium, sunscreen, body oils, chemicals. When the scale is permanent, we remove the old tile, prep the bond beam and set new glass, ceramic or natural stone in waterproof epoxy grout.

Glass mosaicCeramicNatural stoneEpoxy grout

The coping

The cap around the edge. Matched or contrasted with the new tile, it makes the edge read as one piece and keeps water from getting behind the tile.

TravertineWhite IvoryCoordinated with the tile

The deck

Where the backyard actually happens. Travertine, pavers, or stamped and stained concrete, resurfaced or rebuilt, with deck drainage where it’s needed.

Questions

Can you renovate or resurface my existing pool?

Absolutely. We offer complete pool remodeling, including resurfacing, tile replacement, coping upgrades and modern equipment installation.

How long does a pool renovation take?

It depends on the scope. Minor renovations usually take about 2 to 4 weeks; more extensive remodels can take 4 to 8 weeks or longer. You get a detailed timeline at the initial consultation, based on your pool and your design.

What materials do you use?

High-quality materials chosen for your project: tile, pool finishes and decking options. We guide you through the selection for a result that looks good and lasts.

Can you upgrade my equipment during the renovation?

Yes. We specialize in upgrading pumps, filters, heaters and automation systems. It improves performance and can lower energy use and maintenance.

How do I maintain my pool afterwards?

You get maintenance guidelines for your setup: water chemistry, filter cleaning, equipment checks. We also offer maintenance services if you’d rather leave it to us.

Do you do repairs without a full renovation?

Yes. We repair and replace equipment of any brand, from pumps and filters to timers, spa jets and water features.

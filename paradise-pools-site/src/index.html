New pools, designed around your backyard

Our designers and builders create a custom pool that fits your outdoor space, and the way you want to use it.

  1. We get to know you

    From the start, we learn how you live and what you picture for your backyard. Choosing a pool builder is a big decision, so good communication comes first.

  2. You see it in 3D first

    We use the latest 3D design tools to bring your vision to life, and give you a detailed overview of the project before we get to work.

  3. We build it to last

    Our skilled team builds with the highest quality materials and craftsmanship, so your custom pool lasts for years to come.

Remodels, from a fresh finish to a full makeover

Over time, every pool loses its charm. Whether it needs a surface repair or a complete makeover, we work out what you want and need, and turn the old pool into a relaxing outdoor oasis.

  • A new interior finish replaces the old, worn surface, so the pool looks good and feels healthy. We offer a wide range of Marquis Quartz and Stonescape Pebble finishes.

  • New waterline tile and new coping, the stones that cap the pool’s edge.

  • A new deck around the pool, the part of the backyard you walk, sit and lounge on.

  • Pavers and concrete for the deck and the spaces around the water.

  • A custom place to stretch out in the sun, designed for your pool.

  • Upgrades to the equipment that keeps your pool running. Tell us what you have today when you request a quote.

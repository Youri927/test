Can an existing pool be modified?

Yes. Shape, depth, a Baja shelf or a spill-over spa can all change, without ripping out all the existing concrete.

What moves the number

  1. SlopeSteep or uneven ground complicates excavation.
  2. AccessNarrow side yards need specialized planning and equipment.
  3. SoilTough or problematic soil makes excavation harder.
  4. UtilitiesElectrical or gas lines running past 50 feet add cost.
  5. MaterialConcrete, fiberglass or vinyl, and the finish you choose.
  6. FeaturesA spa, a waterfall or a Baja shelf add cost, and value.

Tile, coping, finish, deck.

Those four can completely transform a pool. You pick them from real samples at our showroom in Lewisville.

  • Tile

    The band at the water line, the first thing anyone sees. Anything from bright white to lagoon blue, ceramic or glass.

  • Coping

    The stone edge around the water, the part you sit on. It gives the pool its clean line and ties the backyard together.

  • Finish

    The surface under the water, which sets its color: Quartzscapes plaster, Designer Series plaster, or Stonescapes pebble.

  • Deck

    The ground around the pool, resurfaced, extended or rebuilt.

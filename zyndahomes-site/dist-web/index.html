We plan in winter. We build while you’re away.

Many of our clients spend the summer somewhere else, and condo timelines are short: we start in May and finish by October 1st.

  1. Winter. You’re here.

    We meet, walk through the space and plan it with you: your architect and designer, or local ones we recommend. Then selections and estimating, with your budget in mind.

    Check your building’s rules early. The sooner the details are settled, the sooner work can start.

  2. May. We start.

    Demolition, then framing, rough-ins and finishing details. Our subcontractors come out to the site at the very beginning, so change orders stay rare.

    ZipWalls and plastic sheeting contain the dust; floors, furniture and the building’s common areas stay covered.

  3. June to September. On site, every day.

    We’re there daily, and the site is cleaned up every day. Wherever you spend the summer, you get pictures and progress emails.

  4. October 1st. You’re home.

    The work is done before the season starts.

Condo renovations

A condo renovation happens in a building full of neighbors. We handle HOA approvals, building requirements and logistics, protect the common areas and keep everyone informed.

Buildings we’ve worked in include Le Ciel Towers, The Heron in Pelican Bay, Le Dauphin and Westgate in Naples, and Hideaway Beach Club and Dunnfoire on Marco Island.

What we build

Projects run from $100,000 to more than $7,000,000, depending on plans and scope.

  • Custom homes

    New homes built with your architect and designer, down to every last detail. No team yet? We’ll recommend local architects and interior designers who fit your style.

  • Condo renovations

    Complete renovations in Naples and Marco Island condominiums, with HOA approvals, building requirements and logistics handled.

  • Whole-home renovations

    Full interior renovations of homes and condos, planned around the months you’re away.

  • Kitchens

    Custom cabinetry, stone countertops, premium appliances and high-end finishes.

  • Bathrooms

    Master baths, guest baths and complete renovations.

  • Additions

    More room for your home, planned with your architect and built by the team that’s on site every day.

  • Outdoor kitchens and lanais

    Southwest Florida means sunshine all year. If you’ve got the space, we’ll help you make the most of it, with an outdoor kitchen, a lanai or a place to entertain.
